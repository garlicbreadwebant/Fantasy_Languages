# Volapük pronunciation

Keep a e i o u distinct. ä ö ü are separate vowel letters; ö and ü are rounded front vowels. Stress generally falls on the final syllable. c is commonly voiced like English j and j like English sh in reformed pronunciation. Historical pronunciation conventions may differ.

## Practice

- **Glidis!** — Greetings!
- **Ob binob studan.** — I am a student.
- **Ob drinob vati.** — I drink water.

Synthetic speech may not follow these rules. Written reference and community recordings should take precedence over generated voices.

# Volapük: design and source notes

## Existing language; new learning collection

This project documents Volapük; it does not claim to have designed or standardized it. Fantasy Languages is the collection title; these languages have different historical and community purposes.

## Evidence

[Volapükanef dictionary collection](https://volapuk.temerov.org/Volap%C3%BCkanef/Lingl%C3%A4nap%C3%BCk/v%C3%B6dabuks.php), including the linked Caviness bidirectional vocabulary. That table includes historical forms. This edition labels them and does not claim to be a consistently modern reference.

## Editorial policy

This starter collection includes historical dictionary material. Resolve old versus reformed spellings before using it as a modern course.

Every collection uses the exact 397 English lookup labels frozen from Universal_Languages on October 18, 2026. Shared English labels do not guarantee exact semantic equivalence. Notes distinguish contextual descriptions, historical vocabulary, grammatical constructions, and unresolved equivalents. A dash is an explicit editorial gap or a lack of direct correspondence; it must never be pronounced as a target word.

No unverified form should be described as attested. No language should inherit another language’s grammar. AI assistance must respect these limits and decline to invent missing vocabulary.

## Current coverage

200 supplied learning forms or constructions; 197 unresolved or non-equivalent lookup entries. Supplied entries are a starter collection requiring speaker or historical specialist review, not a claim of authoritative translation.

## Contribute

Provide a source, precise English sense, target form, and any grammatical or historical qualification. Update lexicon.csv and regenerate both dictionaries and the website snapshot.

# Volapük grammar

Nouns mark case: the genitive uses -a; dative -e; accusative -i. Plural -s follows the case ending. Many adjectives end in -ik and infinitives in -ön. Personal endings conjugate verbs: binön becomes binob with first-person ob. Do not append an infinitive unchanged after every subject.

## Read examples with their meanings

| Volapük | English |
|---|---|
| Glidis! | Greetings! |
| Ob binob studan. | I am a student. |
| Ob drinob vati. | I drink water. |

## Limits

This starter collection includes historical dictionary material. Resolve old versus reformed spellings before using it as a modern course.

The 397 English lookup labels include grammatical markers from other constructed languages. Their presence does not imply that Volapük has the same grammatical system.

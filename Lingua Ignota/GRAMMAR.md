# Lingua Ignota grammar

The surviving evidence is primarily a glossary with Latin and German glosses. It does not provide a complete independent conversational grammar. This project teaches isolated documented words and source comparison. It does not invent pronouns or verbs to fill the modern English lookup list.

## Read examples with their meanings

| Lingua Ignota | English |
|---|---|
| Aigonz | God; Latin gloss deus. |
| Peueriz | Father; Latin gloss pater. |
| Maiz | Mother; Latin gloss mater. |

## Limits

Aigonz is not a greeting. The overview displays it as a documented first word. A missing equivalent means this edition has no verified mapping; it does not prove the word never existed.

The 397 English lookup labels include grammatical markers from other constructed languages. Their presence does not imply that Lingua Ignota has the same grammatical system.

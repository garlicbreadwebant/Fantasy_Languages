# Lingua Ignota pronunciation

The manuscript preserves spellings rather than audio. No definitive spoken standard is claimed here. Read words as historical transcriptions and compare manuscript variants. Automated speech is an approximation and cannot recover Hildegard’s voice.

## Practice

- **Aigonz** — God; Latin gloss deus.
- **Peueriz** — Father; Latin gloss pater.
- **Maiz** — Mother; Latin gloss mater.

Synthetic speech may not follow these rules. Written reference and community recordings should take precedence over generated voices.

## Coverage

All 397 English headwords match Universal_Languages. 299 entries supply a learning form or construction; 98 entries are explicitly unresolved or have no direct equivalent. A dash is not a word in this language.

These are isolated lexical practice items rather than invented conversation. Consult the named historical source before forming sentences or borrowing modern community additions.

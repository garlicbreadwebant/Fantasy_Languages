# Solresol grammar

Sudre groups related grammatical forms into word families. Emphasis distinguishes functions such as verb and noun. In his account fasi before an attribute is augmentative and after it superlative. Pronouns include dore and domi. Plural and feminine distinctions use emphasis or lengthening, so do not substitute unmarked singular forms without explanation.

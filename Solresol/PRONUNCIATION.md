# Solresol pronunciation

The seven syllables are do re mi fa sol la si. Speak each syllable distinctly. Text spellings are combinations of these syllables; syllables do not require absolute musical pitch when spoken. Historical elongation and emphasis may encode grammatical distinctions, so plain unmarked text can lose information.

## Practice

- **dore** — I / me.
- **domi** — You.
- **redo** — My / mine.

Synthetic speech may not follow these rules. Written reference and community recordings should take precedence over generated voices.

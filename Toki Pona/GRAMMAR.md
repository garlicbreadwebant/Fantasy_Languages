# Toki Pona grammar

Modifiers follow their head. li separates most subjects from their predicates; omit it after standalone mi and sina. e introduces a direct object. pi regroups modifiers rather than translating every English of. Number and tense are usually contextual. A yes/no question can repeat the predicate as X ala X.

## Read examples with their meanings

| Toki Pona | English |
|---|---|
| toki | Hello. |
| mi moku e kili. | I eat fruit. |
| sina moku ala moku? | Are you eating? |

## Limits

Root meanings are broad. laso covers a blue/green range; soweli does not uniquely mean dog or cat. Do not treat the dictionary’s contextual descriptions as fixed compounds.

The 397 English lookup labels include grammatical markers from other constructed languages. Their presence does not imply that Toki Pona has the same grammatical system.

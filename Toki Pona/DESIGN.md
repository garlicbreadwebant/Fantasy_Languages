# Toki Pona: design and source notes

## Existing language; new learning collection

This project documents Toki Pona; it does not claim to have designed or standardized it. Fantasy Languages is the collection title; these languages have different historical and community purposes.

## Evidence

[Creator’s website](https://www.tokipona.org/) and [published usage data](https://www.tokipona.org/nimi_pu.txt). Root senses were checked against that list. Multiword learning paraphrases are editorial and context dependent.

## Editorial policy

Root meanings are broad. laso covers a blue/green range; soweli does not uniquely mean dog or cat. Do not treat the dictionary’s contextual descriptions as fixed compounds.

Every collection uses the exact 397 English lookup labels frozen from Universal_Languages on October 18, 2026. Shared English labels do not guarantee exact semantic equivalence. Notes distinguish contextual descriptions, historical vocabulary, grammatical constructions, and unresolved equivalents. A dash is an explicit editorial gap or a lack of direct correspondence; it must never be pronounced as a target word.

No unverified form should be described as attested. No language should inherit another language’s grammar. AI assistance must respect these limits and decline to invent missing vocabulary.

## Current coverage

373 supplied learning forms or constructions; 24 unresolved or non-equivalent lookup entries. Supplied entries are a starter collection requiring speaker or historical specialist review, not a claim of authoritative translation.

## Contribute

Provide a source, precise English sense, target form, and any grammatical or historical qualification. Update lexicon.csv and regenerate both dictionaries and the website snapshot.

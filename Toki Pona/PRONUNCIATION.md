# Toki Pona pronunciation

Use five vowel sounds: a as in father; e as in a pure eh; i as in machine; o as a pure oh; u as in rule. Stress the first syllable. j sounds like English y. Native syllables end in a vowel or n.

## Practice

- **toki** — Hello.
- **mi moku e kili.** — I eat fruit.
- **sina moku ala moku?** — Are you eating?

Synthetic speech may not follow these rules. Written reference and community recordings should take precedence over generated voices.

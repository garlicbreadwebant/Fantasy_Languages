## Coverage

All 397 English headwords match Universal_Languages. 373 entries supply a learning form or construction; 24 entries are explicitly unresolved or have no direct equivalent. A dash is not a word in this language.

Root meanings are broad. laso covers a blue/green range; soweli does not uniquely mean dog or cat. Do not treat the dictionary’s contextual descriptions as fixed compounds.

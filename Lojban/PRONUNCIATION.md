# Lojban pronunciation

Vowels a e i o u are pronounced consistently; y is a neutral schwa. c sounds like English sh; j like the sound in measure; x is a voiceless velar fricative. The apostrophe separates vowels with an h-like sound. Stress ordinarily falls on the penultimate syllable of brivla.

## Practice

- **coi** — Hello.
- **mi pinxe lo djacu** — I drink water.
- **xu do pinxe lo djacu** — Do you drink water?

Synthetic speech may not follow these rules. Written reference and community recordings should take precedence over generated voices.

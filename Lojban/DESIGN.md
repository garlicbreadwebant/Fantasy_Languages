# Lojban: design and source notes

## Existing language; new learning collection

This project documents Lojban; it does not claim to have designed or standardized it. Fantasy Languages is the collection title; these languages have different historical and community purposes.

## Evidence

[Official word lists](https://mw.lojban.org/papri/Word_Lists), [gismu list](https://www.lojban.org/publications/wordlists/gismu.txt), and [The Complete Lojban Language](https://lojban.org/publications/cll/cll_v1.1_xhtml-section-chunks/). Predicate glosses are not complete translations without their argument structure.

## Editorial policy

Do not transfer word order or copulas from the sibling languages. Read the full official place structure before combining predicates.

Every collection uses the exact 397 English lookup labels frozen from Universal_Languages on October 18, 2026. Shared English labels do not guarantee exact semantic equivalence. Notes distinguish contextual descriptions, historical vocabulary, grammatical constructions, and unresolved equivalents. A dash is an explicit editorial gap or a lack of direct correspondence; it must never be pronounced as a target word.

No unverified form should be described as attested. No language should inherit another language’s grammar. AI assistance must respect these limits and decline to invent missing vocabulary.

## Current coverage

377 supplied learning forms or constructions; 20 unresolved or non-equivalent lookup entries. Supplied entries are a starter collection requiring speaker or historical specialist review, not a claim of authoritative translation.

## Contribute

Provide a source, precise English sense, target form, and any grammatical or historical qualification. Update lexicon.csv and regenerate both dictionaries and the website snapshot.

# Lojban grammar

A bridi relates arguments (sumti) through a predicate (selbri). mi pinxe lo djacu relates the drinker to water. Every predicate has an ordered place structure; a short English keyword does not describe all its places. pu and ba locate events in the past and future. xu asks whether a proposition is true. .e joins sumti; other levels use other connectives. lo is not simply the English definite article.

## Read examples with their meanings

| Lojban | English |
|---|---|
| coi | Hello. |
| mi pinxe lo djacu | I drink water. |
| xu do pinxe lo djacu | Do you drink water? |

## Limits

Do not transfer word order or copulas from the sibling languages. Read the full official place structure before combining predicates.

The 397 English lookup labels include grammatical markers from other constructed languages. Their presence does not imply that Lojban has the same grammatical system.

# First lessons in Lojban

## 1. Listen and notice

Read the [pronunciation guide](PRONUNCIATION.md). Compare the following items with their English glosses.

| Lojban | English |
|---|---|
| coi | Hello. |
| mi pinxe lo djacu | I drink water. |
| xu do pinxe lo djacu | Do you drink water? |

## 2. Explore the dictionary

Look up water, person, and language. Read each note before assuming that the form is interchangeable with English. A dash means no verified equivalent has been supplied.

## 3. Check your understanding

1. Cover the English glosses and recall their meanings.
2. Identify one feature that differs from English in the [grammar guide](GRAMMAR.md).
3. Find one broad or unresolved dictionary sense and explain why a single-word translation could mislead.

## Answer guide

Use the example table for activity 1. Activity 2 should cite a rule in the grammar guide. Activity 3 should distinguish English lookup coverage from verified target-language vocabulary.

Do not transfer word order or copulas from the sibling languages. Read the full official place structure before combining predicates.
